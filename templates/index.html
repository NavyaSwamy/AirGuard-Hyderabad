    Data transparency

    About AirGuard

    Project purpose

    AirGuard is designed to make Hyderabad air quality easier to understand for students, families, professionals and outdoor enthusiasts.

    Source data

    AirGuard uses 14 OpenCity / CPCB station feeds from 2024–2025 and a separate hourly AQI archive from 2017–2023. The AQI archive has no pollutant breakdown or stated timezone; neither source is live.

    AirGuard Risk Index

    The Risk Index is a user-facing interpretation derived from measured pollutants. It is not an official AQI issued by a regulatory body.
